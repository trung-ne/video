# Abstracted style guide

## Source observation

The project frames mix simple hand-drawn cat overlays, flat stock-vector backgrounds, diagrams, photos, and large Vietnamese subtitles. The reusable character language is the relevant part; screenshots, ads, celebrity photos, logos, and subtitles are not style targets.

## Visual grammar to preserve

- Thick near-black contour with mild wobble and rounded joins.
- Large primitive shapes with flat color and almost no shading.
- Sparse face: dot or dash eyes, tiny nose, short mouth marks.
- Comedic deadpan acting; gestures and props carry meaning.
- Thumbnail-first readability and strong figure/background separation.
- Costumes are simplified into two or three recognizable shapes.
- Scenes may pair a crude character with a cleaner flat background.

## Originality guardrails

Treat the frames as a vocabulary, not a model sheet. Do not reproduce the purple cat, its wide horizontal head, central white oval muzzle, white belly patch, stick limbs, or exact facial placement as a bundle. A new lead must differ in all of these dimensions:

1. Silhouette: use a compact pear, soft trapezoid, or short angular torso rather than the same tall bean.
2. Palette: avoid purple as the dominant body color; prefer teal, rust, mustard, coral, slate blue, or warm gray.
3. Face: use a small off-center muzzle patch, muzzle split, or no muzzle patch rather than the same broad white oval.
4. Proportions: change head-to-body ratio and limb length.
5. Identity: add an original asymmetric marking and a modest accessory.
6. Anatomy: give the design a visible tail or distinctive ear treatment.

Do not imitate a named living artist or studio. Describe visible formal properties only.

## Recommended lead concept

Use a squat teal cat with a soft trapezoid torso, short legs, one folded ear, a cream crescent muzzle offset to one side, three rust cheek stripes, a curved visible tail, and a small mustard neckerchief. Keep the expression calmly skeptical. This concept retains low-fi editorial humor but is not a recolor of the source lead.
